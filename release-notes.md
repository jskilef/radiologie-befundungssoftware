Report Studio v1.5.0 adds experimental MedGemma 4B text-only browser CPU/WebGPU inference, local model-folder loading, and exact model download links.

Local folders contain configuration, tokenizers, ONNX weights/external data or MLC shards plus a matching compiled library. Model files stay in the browser and are not saved in encrypted settings. Runtime assets still require jsDelivr. MedGemma is a community conversion of the original 4B model; full inference and German radiology quality remain unverified.
